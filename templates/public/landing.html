About HRyUp

The name HRyUp combines "HR" (Human Resources) and "Up" representing improvement, progress, and helping businesses move forward. Pronounced similarly to "hurry up", it reflects our commitment to providing timely, continuous, and highly professional HR support.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, reliable, and affordable virtual human resource support to MSMEs in Las Piñas City by simplifying HR tasks, reducing administrative workload, and helping business owners manage their employees more effectively.

Our Vision

To become a trusted virtual HR support partner for MSMEs in Las Piñas City by providing accessible, reliable, and practical HR services that support effective business operations.

Our Core Principles

  • Confidentiality & Privacy

    Strict protection of client and employee information, adhering to legitimate business purposes.

  • Professionalism

    Respectful communication and responsible execution of duties in all stakeholder interactions.

  • Accountability & Quality

    Accurate, timely task completion with rigorous review of HR documents and records.

  • Fairness

    Recruitment and assistance based strictly on job-related qualifications and requirements.

Our Core Services

Comprehensive virtual HR support delivered through a secure digital platform.

Recruitment Assistance

We handle job postings, application collection, CV screening, and initial candidate shortlisting based on your requirements. You retain the final hiring decision.

Onboarding Support

Organized orientation processes for newly hired employees, including document preparation and dissemination of company procedures.

201 Files Management

Secure, centralized digital maintenance of employee personnel records. Access to confidential records is strictly limited to authorized personnel.

Attendance & Leave

Digital collection of employee time, schedule, and attendance data with structured workflows for reviewing and approving leave requests.

Payroll Assistance

Aggregation and organization of attendance, approved leave, and compensation inputs for accurate and timely payroll preparation.

HR Helpdesk Support

A dedicated communication channel for employee queries regarding procedures, attendance, and benefits, with escalation protocols for management decisions.
